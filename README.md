# Zhaopeng Ding

## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Cryptographic Science and Technology at Beijing Electronic Science and Technology Institute (BESTI).

My research focuses on cryptanalysis and its mathematical foundations, with a particular emphasis on Coppersmith's method and its applications.

## Research Interests

- Lattice-based cryptanalysis
- Coppersmith's method
- Computational number theory
- Cryptographic hash functions
